EQUIPMENT: KRONES Modulfill VFS
ERROR CODE: ERR-TEMP-901
DESCRIPTION: Main drive shaft bearing overheating beyond standard operational range.
ROOT CAUSE: Lubrication seal degradation leading to high mechanical friction.
CORRECTIVE ACTION:
1. Immediately throttle line speed to 50% capacity.
2. Inspect primary lubrication lines for seal pressure drop.
3. Replace thermal grease and bearing assembly if ambient temperature exceeds 90C.

ERROR CODE: ERR-VIB-402
DESCRIPTION: Abnormal vibration detected in rotary carousels.
ROOT CAUSE: Unbalanced load distribution or loose mounting bolts on sub-frame.
CORRECTIVE ACTION:
1. Halt bottle feed queue.
2. Execute torque check on mounting bolts (Standard: 45 Nm).
3. Recalibrate vibration sensors using diagnostic dashboard.
